About Kofi Mensah

Kofi is the kind of person who reads job descriptions the way other people read movie reviews — looking for what they are really saying between the lines. He joined SchollyJob because he wanted to write the career guides he wishes had existed when he was starting out: honest about how hard things actually are, specific about what actually works. Outside of work he follows football with an unhealthy level of intensity, cooks elaborate weekend meals that rarely go according to plan, and is slowly working his way through every jazz album ever recorded.
