We Help You Find the Opportunity That Changes Everything

SchollyJob is a free resource covering scholarships, fellowships, jobs, and study abroad programs for students and professionals worldwide.

Our Mission

SchollyJob was built on a simple frustration: the best information about fully funded scholarships, competitive fellowships, and high-paying international jobs is scattered across dozens of fragmented, often outdated sources. Meanwhile, a growing industry of consultants and fake portals charges money to provide access to programs that are completely free to apply for directly.

We created SchollyJob to fix that. Every guide on this site is free, regularly updated, and written to give you the specific, actionable information you actually need to apply successfully — not to upsell you on consulting services or referral programs.

Our editorial team researches every program we cover, verifies that it is currently active, checks the official application portal, and writes with the specific audience in mind: motivated students and professionals who deserve honest, direct guidance rather than recycled surface-level content.

What We Cover

  • Fully Funded Scholarships — Chevening, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, Mastercard Foundation, and 50+ more programs with detailed eligibility breakdowns and application strategy.
  • International Jobs — UN system roles, NGO positions, remote jobs paying $5,000+/month, jobs in Europe with visa sponsorship, and high-paying opportunities in Canada.
  • Study Abroad — Free tuition countries, budget guides, student visa walkthroughs for Germany, UK, USA, and Canada, and practical cost-of-living breakdowns.
  • Fellowships — Fulbright, Gates Cambridge, YALI, Mandela Washington, UNESCO, World Bank, and research fellowships for African scholars.
  • Career Development — CV writing, LinkedIn optimization, salary negotiation, interview preparation, and the skill paths that lead to six-figure remote income.

Our Editorial Principles

Every article on SchollyJob is written against four non-negotiable standards:

  • Verified and current. We check official program websites before publishing and update content when programs change. If a program has closed or changed its requirements, we say so.
  • Specific over generic. We give you exact deadlines, exact stipend amounts, exact portal URLs, and honest assessments of what selection committees are actually looking for. We do not write vague inspirational content that leaves you no better informed than before you arrived.
  • No paid placements. SchollyJob does not accept payments from scholarship programs, universities, or employers to feature or positively present their programs. Our coverage decisions are driven by what is genuinely useful to our readers.
  • Free forever. Everything on SchollyJob is and will remain free. We will never charge you to read our content, access our resources, or use our guides.

How SchollyJob Is Funded

SchollyJob is supported by display advertising and occasional affiliate partnerships with educational platforms and tools we have personally vetted and found useful. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial decisions. These partnerships do not influence our editorial decisions. We do not accept payment to feature any program, employer, or institution, and we do not use sponsored content that is not clearly labeled as such.

If you find SchollyJob useful, the best way to support us is to share our articles with people who need them. Every reader we help find a legitimate opportunity is the reason this site exists.

Found an Error? Want to Contribute?

Scholarship and program details change. If you find outdated information, a broken link, or an error in any of our guides, please let us know through our contact page. We take accuracy seriously and will verify and correct any reported errors promptly.

If you are an expert in international scholarships, development careers, or study abroad and want to contribute to SchollyJob, we are always interested in working with people who have direct, verifiable experience in the programs we cover. Reach out through the contact page with details about your background and the topics you would like to cover.
