Realistically, 18-36 months from zero for most people — 6-12 months building a skill, 3-6 months building a portfolio and landing first work, then 12-18 months of experience before the higher-paying roles become accessible. Anyone promising this in 3 months is selling a course. Our full roadmap gives honest stage-by-stage timelines.
AI/ML engineering ($90K-$140K entry level at major tech companies), cybersecurity ($60K-$200K depending on seniority), cloud computing certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure — strong premiums across all tech roles), and data analysis with Python/SQL ($50K-$130K). Our skills guide gives realistic learning timelines for each.
Use market data as your anchor — not personal financial need. Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, Levels.fyi, and sector surveys all provide reference ranges. Three data points from verified sources is a strong negotiating position. The timing rule: negotiate only after receiving a written offer, not during the interview process. Our full guide covers the psychology and exact language.
Not your job title — that tells recruiters nothing useful. Use a skills-based headline: 'Digital Marketing Specialist driving 2-5x ROAS for B2B SaaS companies' instead of 'Marketing Manager at Company X'. Skills-based headlines are indexed by LinkedIn's recruiter search tool and surface you in relevant searches. Job-title headlines are not meaningfully searchable beyond basic keyword matching.
In most of the world, CV and resume are used interchangeably for the same 1-2 page professional summary. In the US and Canada specifically, a 'CV' typically refers to a longer academic document listing all publications, research, and presentations. Our CV guide covers both formats with specific advice for international job markets.
